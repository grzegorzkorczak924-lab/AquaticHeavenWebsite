About Aquatic Heaven

Aquatic Heaven is an aquatic management game focused on creating and managing your own aquatic paradise.

Build habitats, discover aquatic life and create a place where marine and freshwater animals can thrive.

What is Aquatic Heaven?

Aquatic Heaven is a game about aquatic life, creativity and management.

The goal is to create your own aquatic world while learning about the incredible animals that live in our oceans, rivers and lakes.

About the Developer
