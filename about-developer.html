About the Lead Developer

Grzegorz

Hi! I'm Grzegorz, the Lead Developer of Aquatic Heaven.

Aquatic Heaven is a project created from my passion for aquatic life, game development, and the natural world.

As the Lead Developer, I work on the game's programming, design, mechanics, species selection, and overall direction.

My Vision

My goal is to create an aquatic management game where players can discover and care for a wide variety of aquatic animals while building their own underwater world.

What I'm Working On

I'm currently developing the game's aquarium systems, touch controls, animal habitats, and other features that will shape Aquatic Heaven.
